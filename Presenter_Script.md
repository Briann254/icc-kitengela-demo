# Presenting the ICC Kitengela Church OS Demo
**Goal:** Get leadership approval to go all-in. ~6–8 minutes. One screen. Hope, transparency, ease, and "wow, that's us."

---

## The 45-second open (before you click anything)
Project the **intro screen** — the one that says "*You belong here. Connecting people to God.*" It already looks like THEM, not a generic product.

> "We don't want to sell you software. We want to show you what your church already is — 2,000+ members, three services, active ministries — working in one place, automatically.
> Everything you see tonight runs live. It's already styled in your brand, uses your verse, your services, your M-Pesa Paybill. The only thing sample about it is the names on the screen.

## Tap: **View the Admin Console**

### 1. Dashboard (15s)
> "This is what a Sunday looks like in one screen. Attendance, giving, kids checked in, prayer requests — **instead of five Excel sheets, one dashboard.**"

**Point to:** Members 2,318 · Giving this week KES 1.24M · Kids checked in 312.

### 2. Kids Check-in (now 0-to-3 in) — *the safety win first*
> "This is Next Gen. A parent drops a child, we print a code, and the **guardian's phone gets a matching pick-up prompt**. No paper, no mix-ups, every volunteer background-checked and on record."

> "Every parent asks one question: *is my child safe?* Now the answer is written into the system."

### 3. Giving & Finance — *the transparency moment*
> "Tithes, offerings, pledges are auto-caught from **M-Pesa Paybill 567935**. Finance sees the ledger; leadership sees the totals; **every member can get a one-click annual giving statement.** Role-based access means the kids director never sees the money — and the audit log is unchangeable."

**Say this slowly:** "This is where we bring full transparency — the thing you asked for."

### 4. Member directory
> "Every member, guest, ministry, and cell group, searchable. New guests get picked up for follow-up instead of lost in a notebook."

### 5. Member App (tap it)
> "Your congregation downloads 'ICC Kitengela.' Give one-tap, watch online, RSVP to events, check in their kids, send a prayer request — **from their pocket.**"

---

## The close (30 seconds) — ask for the decision
> "This is one system replacing the spreadsheets forever. We've shown you the possibility — we're ready to build it on **your real records**, module by module, starting with the one you choose to make your life easier first. **What's the website's #1 daily pain — members or finance?** We start there."

Then shut up and let them talk. **The demo did the selling. You just choose.**

---

## Rules for the room
- **Don't read.** Speak like you believe it (you do). 
- **Let them click.** Put the laptop on the table — people commit to what they touch.
- **Don't overwhelm.** Show 5 screens, not 10.
- **The ask is ONE question:** "Which module do we start with?" — not "do you approve?"
- If they name a module → say "Perfect. That becomes our demo number two, built on real data." That's the demo.

---

## What convinced them
- **It's their brand** (dark + gold #C69239, Joshua 1:9, Paybill 567935) → *"this is us"*
- **It looks high-end** → *"this makes us look cool"*
- **It shows the exact pain being killed** → *"there's the spreadsheet I've been fighting"*
- **It's clickable, not a slide** → *"they actually built this"*
