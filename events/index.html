About ICC Kitengela

Assemblies of God · Connecting People to God, Empowering Families

Who We Are

International Christian Centre Kitengela is an Assemblies of God church located along Namanga Road in Kitengela town, Kenya. We are a family of believers committed to passionate worship, deep biblical discipleship, community transformation, and raising the next generation of godly leaders.

Our Vision

To be a vibrant, Christ-centered, transforming church that connects every person to God, equips families, and impacts the community with God's love.

Our Mission

To preach the uncompromised Word of God, foster authentic relationships in home cells, and mobilize believers into effective kingdom ministry.

ICC Kitengela Discipleship & Sermon Notes

Kingdom Greatness — Study Guide

Scripture: Matthew 18:1-5 Preacher: Pst. Alice Kitonga

1. The Childlike Posture of Faith

In the Kingdom of God, greatness is measured not by earthly status, leverage, or prominence, but by humility and complete dependence on the Father. A child does not boast of self-sufficiency; they look entirely to their parent for sustenance and guidance.

2. Unmasking Spiritual Pride

The disciples disputed among themselves about who would be the greatest. Jesus responded by positioning a small child in their midst. True spiritual maturity manifests as servant-hearted posture in your family, Safari group, and workplace.

Safari Group Reflection Questions

  • In what areas of your career or family are you struggling to surrender control to God?
  • How can our fellowship group hold each other accountable to servant leadership this week?
