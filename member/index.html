Our Heritage & Mission

About ICC Kitengela

Assemblies of God · Connecting People to God, Empowering Families

Who We Are

International Christian Centre Kitengela is an Assemblies of God church located along Namanga Road in Kitengela town, Kenya. We are a family of believers committed to passionate worship, deep biblical discipleship, community transformation, and raising the next generation of godly leaders.

Our Vision

To be a vibrant, Christ-centered, transforming church that connects every person to God, equips families, and impacts the community with God's love.

Our Mission

To preach the uncompromised Word of God, foster authentic relationships in home cells, and mobilize believers into effective kingdom ministry.
